# Code of Conduct

[Polski](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.pl.md) · English

## Our pledge

We want Cipher Lab to be a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, nationality or beliefs. Puzzles are more fun together.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good intent and ask when something is unclear.
- Keep discussions on topic and in the spirit of learning.
- Do not spoil puzzle answers in public places unless the puzzle author has solved or retired it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or discrimination.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, or puzzles and comments designed to harm or deceive others.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces: the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that break this code, and may warn or ban people who do. Report problems by contacting the maintainers privately, for example through the contact details on their GitHub profiles. Reports are handled confidentially and fairly.
